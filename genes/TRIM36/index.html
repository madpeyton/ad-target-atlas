TRIM36

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000152503.

The protein encoded by this gene is a member of the tripartite motif (TRIM) family. The TRIM motif includes three zinc-binding domains, a RING, a B-box type 1 and a B-box type 2, and a coiled-coil region. Multiple alternatively spliced transcript variants that encode different protein isoforms have been described for this gene. [provided by RefSeq, Jul 2008].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase which mediates ubiquitination and subsequent proteasomal degradation of target proteins including CTNNB1, FOXA2 and AKT1 (PubMed:35768649, PubMed:37875418, PubMed:41323265). Involved in chromosome segregation and cell cycle regulation (PubMed:28087737). May play a role in the acrosome reaction and fertilization.

Show more (4 more sentences)

Inhibits lung fibroblast proliferation, migration, and differentiation. Mechanistically, mediates 'Lys-48'-linked polyubiquitination of phospho-AKT1, leading to its proteasomal degradation and consequent inhibition of AKT signaling (PubMed:41323265). Promotes apoptosis via inhibition of the Wnt/beta-catenin signaling pathway (PubMed:35768649). Regulates ferroptosis by ubiquitinating and degrading FOXA2 (PubMed:37875418).
