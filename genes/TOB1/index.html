TOB1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000141232.

This gene encodes a member of the transducer of erbB-2 /B-cell translocation gene protein family. Members of this family are anti-proliferative factors that have the potential to regulate cell growth. The encoded protein may function as a tumor suppressor. Alternate splicing results in multiple transcript variants. [provided by RefSeq, Aug 2011].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Anti-proliferative protein; the function is mediated by association with deadenylase subunits of the CCR4-NOT complex (PubMed:23236473, PubMed:8632892). Mediates CPEB3-accelerated mRNA deadenylation by binding to CPEB3 and recruiting CNOT7 which leads to target mRNA deadenylation and decay (PubMed:21336257).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (P50616), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
