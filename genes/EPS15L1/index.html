EPS15L1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000127527.

Enables cadherin binding activity. Predicted to be involved in endocytosis and endosomal transport. Located in membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Seems to be a constitutive component of clathrin-coated pits that is required for receptor-mediated endocytosis. Involved in endocytosis of integrin beta-1 (ITGB1) and transferrin receptor (TFR); internalization of ITGB1 as DAB2-dependent cargo but not TFR seems to require association with DAB2.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9UBC2), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 58513 · UniProt Q9UBC2 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
