NSUN6

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000241058.

Enables tRNA (cytosine-5-)-methyltransferase activity and tRNA binding activity. Involved in tRNA C5-cytosine methylation. Located in cytoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

S-adenosyl-L-methionine-dependent methyltransferase that specifically methylates the C5 position of cytosine 72 in tRNA(Thr)(TGT) and tRNA(Cys)(GCA) (PubMed:26160102, PubMed:27703015, PubMed:28531330). In vitro also methylates tRNA(Thr)(AGT) (PubMed:26160102, PubMed:27703015). Methylation requires, in the acceptor stem region, the presence of the 3'-CCA terminus, the target site C72, the discriminator base U73, and the second and third base pairs (2:71 and 3:70) in the tRNA substrates (PubMed:26160102, PubMed:27703015).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q8TEA1), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 221078 · UniProt Q8TEA1 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
