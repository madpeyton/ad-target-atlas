Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000198919.

Enables several functions, including phosphatase binding activity; polyubiquitin modification-dependent protein binding activity; and ubiquitin-protein transferase activity. Involved in protein polyubiquitination. Located in cytoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

E3 Ubiquitin ligase proteins mediate ubiquitination and subsequent proteasomal degradation of target proteins. E3 ubiquitin ligases accept ubiquitin from an E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme in the form of a thioester and then directly transfers the ubiquitin to targeted substrates. Able to specifically bind RNA.
