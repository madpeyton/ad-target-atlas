UBXN7

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000163960.

Enables ubiquitin binding activity and ubiquitin protein ligase binding activity. Located in nuclear body. Part of VCP-NPL4-UFD1 AAA ATPase complex. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Ubiquitin-binding adapter that links a subset of NEDD8-associated cullin ring ligases (CRLs) to the segregase VCP/p97, to regulate turnover of their ubiquitination substrates.
