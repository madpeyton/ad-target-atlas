ZNF512B

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000196700.

Predicted to enable DNA binding activity and metal ion binding activity. Located in nucleoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Involved in transcriptional regulation by repressing gene expression (PubMed:39460621). Associates with the nucleosome remodeling and histone deacetylase (NuRD) complex, which promotes transcriptional repression by histone deacetylation and nucleosome remodeling (PubMed:39460621). Sequence-specific DNA-binding protein that recognizes repetitive and non-consecutive TTC sequences in pericentric repeat and initiate heterochromatin formation through interaction with SUV39H1/2 methyltransferases which catalyze histone H3K9 methylation (By similarity).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q96KM6), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
