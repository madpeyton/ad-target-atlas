PRELID3A

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000141391.

Enables phosphatidic acid transfer activity. Involved in phospholipid transport. Predicted to be active in mitochondrial intermembrane space. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

In vitro, the TRIAP1:PRELID3A complex mediates the transfer of phosphatidic acid (PA) between liposomes and probably functions as a PA transporter across the mitochondrion intermembrane space. Phosphatidic acid import is required for cardiolipin (CL) synthesis in the mitochondrial inner membrane.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q96N28), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
