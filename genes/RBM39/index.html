RBM39

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000131051.

This gene encodes a member of the U2AF65 family of proteins. The encoded protein is found in the nucleus, where it co-localizes with core spliceosomal proteins. It has been shown to play a role in both steroid hormone receptor-mediated transcription and alternative splicing, and it is also a transcriptional coregulator of the viral oncoprotein v-Rel. Multiple transcript variants have been observed for this gene. A related pseudogene has been identified on chromosome X. [provided by RefSeq, Aug 2011].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

RNA-binding protein that acts as a pre-mRNA splicing factor (PubMed:15694343, PubMed:24795046, PubMed:28302793, PubMed:28437394, PubMed:31271494). Acts by promoting exon inclusion via regulation of exon cassette splicing (PubMed:31271494). Also acts as a transcriptional coactivator for steroid nuclear receptors ESR1/ER-alpha and ESR2/ER-beta, and JUN/AP-1, independently of the pre-mRNA splicing factor activity (By similarity).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q14498), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
