ABHD8

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000127220.

This gene is upstream of, and in a head-to-head orientation with the gene for the mitochondrial ribosomal protein L34. The predicted protein contains alpha/beta hydrolase fold and secretory lipase domains. [provided by RefSeq, Jul 2008].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Negatively regulates NLRP3-driven inflammation (PubMed:39225180). Promotes NLRP3 degradation through the chaperone-mediated autophagy (CMA) pathway, hence attenuating inflammasome activation and IL1B secretion. Acts by recruiting palmitoyltransferase ZDHHC12 to NLRP3, facilitating NLRP3 palmitoylation and subsequent degradation (PubMed:39225180).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q96I13), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 79575 · UniProt Q96I13 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
