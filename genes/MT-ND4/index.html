MT-ND4

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000198886.

Enables NADH dehydrogenase (ubiquinone) activity. Involved in mitochondrial electron transport, NADH to ubiquinone and mitochondrial respiratory chain complex I assembly. Part of mitochondrial respiratory chain complex I. Implicated in Leber hereditary optic neuropathy; Parkinson's disease; macular degeneration; and schizophrenia. Biomarker of Alzheimer's disease. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Core subunit of the mitochondrial membrane respiratory chain NADH dehydrogenase (Complex I) which catalyzes electron transfer from NADH through the respiratory chain, using ubiquinone as an electron acceptor (PubMed:15250827, PubMed:8344246, PubMed:8644732). Essential for the catalytic activity and assembly of complex I (PubMed:15250827, PubMed:8344246, PubMed:8644732).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (P03905), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
