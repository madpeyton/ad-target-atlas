NAA35

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000135040.

Involved in negative regulation of apoptotic process. Located in cytosol; nucleoplasm; and plasma membrane. Part of NatC complex. Colocalizes with polysome. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Auxillary component of the N-terminal acetyltransferase C (NatC) complex which catalyzes acetylation of N-terminal methionine residues (PubMed:19398576, PubMed:37891180). N-terminal acetylation protects proteins from ubiquitination and degradation by the N-end rule pathway (PubMed:37891180). Involved in regulation of apoptosis and proliferation of smooth muscle cells (PubMed:19398576).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q5VZE5), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 60560 · UniProt Q5VZE5 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
