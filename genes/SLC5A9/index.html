SLC5A9

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000117834.

Predicted to enable glucose:sodium symporter activity. Predicted to be involved in sodium ion transport. Located in extracellular exosome. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Electrogenic Na(+)-coupled sugar symporter that may play a primary role in D-mannose and possibly D-fructose and D-glucose transport at the plasma membrane. Transporter activity is driven by a transmembrane Na(+) electrochemical gradient set by the Na(+)/K(+) pump. Exclusively recognizes sugar substrates having a pyranose ring with an axial hydroxyl group on carbon 2.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q2M3M2), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
