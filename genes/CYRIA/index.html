CYRIA

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000197872. Also called: FAM49A (symbols used by the datasets).

Predicted to enable small GTPase binding activity. Predicted to be involved in regulation of actin filament polymerization. Predicted to be located in membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

May negatively regulate RAC1 signaling and RAC1-driven cytoskeletal remodeling (Probable). May regulate chemotaxis, cell migration and epithelial polarization by controlling the polarity, plasticity, duration and extent of protrusions (Probable).
