ZNF536

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000198597.

The protein encoded by this gene is a highly conserved zinc finger protein. The encoded protein is most abundant in brain, where it negatively regulates neuronal differentiation. [provided by RefSeq, Sep 2015].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Transcriptional repressor that negatively regulates neuron differentiation by repressing retinoic acid-induced gene transcription (PubMed:19398580). Binds and interrupts RARA from binding to retinoic acid response elements (RARE) composed of tandem 5'-AGGTCA-3' sites known as DR1-DR5 (PubMed:19398580). Recognizes and binds 2 copies of the core DNA sequence 5'-CCCCCA-3' (PubMed:14621294).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (O15090), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
