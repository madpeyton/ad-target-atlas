SLAIN2

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000109171.

Involved in cytoplasmic microtubule organization; microtubule nucleation; and positive regulation of microtubule polymerization. Located in centrosome and cytosol. Colocalizes with microtubule plus-end. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Binds to the plus end of microtubules and regulates microtubule dynamics and microtubule organization. Promotes cytoplasmic microtubule nucleation and elongation. Required for normal structure of the microtubule cytoskeleton during interphase.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9P270), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
