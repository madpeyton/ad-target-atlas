ARB2A

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000113391. Also called: FAM172A (symbols used by the datasets).

Predicted to contribute to siRNA binding activity. Predicted to be involved in heterochromatin assembly by small RNA; neural crest cell development; and regulation of alternative mRNA splicing, via spliceosome. Located in endoplasmic reticulum. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Plays a role in the regulation of alternative splicing, by interacting with AGO2 and CHD7. Seems to be required for stabilizing protein-protein interactions at the chromatin-spliceosome interface. May have hydrolase activity.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q8WUF8), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 83989 · UniProt Q8WUF8 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
