CCDC92

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000119242.

Enables identical protein binding activity. Located in centriole; centrosome; and nucleoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Interferon-stimulated protein that plays a role in innate immunity. Strongly inhibits ebolavirus transcription and replication. Forms a complex with viral RNA-bound nucleocapsid NP and thereby prevents the transport of NP to the cell surface.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q53HC0), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
