TSLIG2

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000135974. Also called: C2orf49 (symbols used by the datasets).

Predicted to be involved in embryonic morphogenesis. Located in nucleus. Part of tRNA-splicing ligase complex. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Accessory subunit of the tRNA-splicing ligase complex that acts by directly joining spliced tRNA halves to mature-sized tRNAs by incorporating the precursor-derived splice junction phosphate into the mature tRNA as a canonical 3',5'-phosphodiester. May act as an RNA ligase with broad substrate specificity and may function toward other RNAs.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9BVC5), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
