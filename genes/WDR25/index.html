WDR25

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000176473.

This gene encodes a protein containing 7 WD repeats. WD repeats are approximately 30 to 40-amino acid domains containing several conserved residues, typically having a Tryptophan-Aspartic acid dipeptide (WD) at the C-terminal end. WD domains are involved in protein-protein interactions in a variety of cellular processes, including cell cycle progression, signal transduction, apoptosis, and gene regulation. Alternative splicing results in multiple transcript variants. [provided by RefSeq, Apr 2017].
