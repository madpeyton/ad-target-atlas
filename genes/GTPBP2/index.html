GTPBP2

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000172432.

GTP-binding proteins, or G proteins, constitute a superfamily capable of binding GTP or GDP. G proteins are activated by binding GTP and are inactivated by hydrolyzing GTP to GDP. This general mechanism enables G proteins to perform a wide range of biologic activities.[supplied by OMIM, Jan 2003].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Involved in the rescue of ribosome stalling due to the presence of non-functional tRNA (By similarity). Has very low GTP-binding activity (PubMed:30108131).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9BX10), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
