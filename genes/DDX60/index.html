DDX60

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000137628.

DEAD box proteins, characterized by the conserved motif Asp-Glu-Ala-Asp (DEAD), are putative RNA helicases which are implicated in a number of cellular procsses involving RNA binding and alteration of RNA secondary structure. This gene encodes a DEXD/H box RNA helicase that functions as an antiviral factor and promotes RIG-I-like receptor-mediated signaling. [provided by RefSeq, Apr 2017].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Positively regulates RIGI- and IFIH1/MDA5-dependent type I interferon and interferon inducible gene expression in response to viral infection. Binds ssRNA, dsRNA and dsDNA and can promote the binding of RIGI to dsRNA. Exhibits antiviral activity against hepatitis C virus and vesicular stomatitis virus (VSV).

Show more (1 more sentence)

Selectively reduces infectious titers of viruses that depend on type II IRES-mediated translation by modulating ribosome occupancy on type II IRES-containing viral mRNAs, without affecting type I IRES-mediated translation (PubMed:36256515).
