KRCC1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000172086.

Regulates CHEK1-mediated cell cycle checkpoint (PubMed:36243983). Facilitates efficient recombination and homologous recombination repair (PubMed:36243983). Required for proper S-phase progression and mitotic entry (PubMed:36243983).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9NPI7), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
