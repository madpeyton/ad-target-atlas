ELP2

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000134759.

The protein encoded by this gene is a core subunit of the elongator complex, a histone acetyltransferase complex that associates with RNA polymerase II. In addition to histone acetylation, the encoded protein effects transcriptional elongation and may help remodel chromatin. [provided by RefSeq, May 2016].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Component of the elongator complex which is required for multiple tRNA modifications, including mcm5U (5-methoxycarbonylmethyl uridine), mcm5s2U (5-methoxycarbonylmethyl-2-thiouridine), and ncm5U (5-carbamoylmethyl uridine) (PubMed:29332244). The elongator complex catalyzes the formation of carboxymethyluridine in the wobble base at position 34 in tRNAs (PubMed:29332244).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q6IA86), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
