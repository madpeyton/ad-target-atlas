CMTR1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000137200.

Enables mRNA (nucleoside-2'-O-)-methyltransferase activity. Involved in 7-methylguanosine mRNA capping and cap1 mRNA methylation. Located in nucleoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

S-adenosyl-L-methionine-dependent methyltransferase that mediates mRNA cap1 2'-O-ribose methylation to the 5'-cap structure of mRNAs. Methylates the ribose of the first nucleotide of a m(7)GpppG-capped mRNA and small nuclear RNA (snRNA) to produce m(7)GpppRm (cap1). Displays a preference for cap0 transcripts.

Show more (2 more sentences)

Cap1 modification is linked to higher levels of translation. May be involved in the interferon response pathway.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q8N1G2), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
