SLC38A10

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000157637.

Predicted to enable amino acid transmembrane transporter activity. Predicted to be involved in amino acid transmembrane transport. Predicted to act upstream of or within bone development. Located in Golgi apparatus. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Facilitates bidirectional transport of amino acids. May act as a glutamate sensor that regulates glutamate-glutamine cycle and mTOR signaling in the brain. The transport mechanism remains to be elucidated.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9HBR0), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
