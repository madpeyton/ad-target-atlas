NAA20

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000173418.

NAT5 is a component of N-acetyltransferase complex B (NatB). Human NatB performs cotranslational N(alpha)-terminal acetylation of methionine residues when they are followed by asparagine (Starheim et al., 2008 [PubMed 18570629]).[supplied by OMIM, Apr 2009].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Catalytic subunit of the NatB complex which catalyzes acetylation of the N-terminal methionine residues of peptides beginning with Met-Asp, Met-Glu, Met-Asn and Met-Gln (PubMed:34230638). Proteins with cell cycle functions are overrepresented in the pool of NatB substrates. Required for maintaining the structure and function of actomyosin fibers and for proper cellular migration.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (P61599), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
