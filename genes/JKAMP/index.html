JKAMP

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000050130.

Enables ubiquitin protein ligase binding activity. Involved in ubiquitin-dependent ERAD pathway. Predicted to be located in endoplasmic reticulum membrane. Predicted to be integral component of membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Regulates the duration of MAPK8 activity in response to various stress stimuli (By similarity). Facilitates the degradation of misfolded endoplasmic reticulum (ER) proteins through the recruitment of components of the proteasome and endoplasmic reticulum-associated degradation (ERAD) system (PubMed:18784250). Is required for proper degradation of prosaposin receptor GPR37 (PubMed:41643666).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9P055), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
