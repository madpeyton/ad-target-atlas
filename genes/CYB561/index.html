CYB561

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000008283.

Predicted to enable transmembrane monodehydroascorbate reductase activity. Predicted to be involved in ascorbate homeostasis. Predicted to be located in chromaffin granule membrane. Predicted to be active in lysosomal membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Transmembrane reductase that probably uses ascorbate as an electron donor to reduce Fe(3+) into Fe(2+) and could play a role in iron transport. It is also able to reduce extracellular monodehydro-L-ascorbate and could be involved in the regeneration and homeostasis within secretory vesicles of ascorbate which in turn provides reducing equivalents needed to support the activity of intravesicular enzymes.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (P49447), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
