HP1BP3

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000127483.

Enables DNA binding activity and nucleosome binding activity. Involved in several processes, including cellular response to hypoxia; heterochromatin organization; and regulation of nucleus size. Located in chromosome and nuclear speck. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Component of heterochromatin that maintains heterochromatin integrity during G1/S progression and regulates the duration of G1 phase to critically influence cell proliferative capacity (PubMed:24830416). Mediates chromatin condensation during hypoxia, leading to increased tumor cell viability, radio-resistance, chemo-resistance and self-renewal (PubMed:25100860).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q5SSJ5), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
