SDCCAG8

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000054282.

This gene encodes a centrosome associated protein. This protein may be involved in organizing the centrosome during interphase and mitosis. Mutations in this gene are associated with retinal-renal ciliopathy. [provided by RefSeq, Oct 2010].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Plays a role in the establishment of cell polarity and epithelial lumen formation (By similarity). Also plays an essential role in ciliogenesis and subsequent Hedgehog signaling pathway that requires the presence of intact primary cilia for pathway activation. Mechanistically, interacts with and mediates RABEP2 centrosomal localization which is critical for ciliogenesis (PubMed:27224062).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q86SQ7), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
