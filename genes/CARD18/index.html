CARD18

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000255501.

Enables CARD domain binding activity and caspase binding activity. Involved in inhibition of cysteine-type endopeptidase activity; negative regulation of interleukin-1 beta production; and negative regulation of protein binding activity. Part of protein-containing complex. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Inhibits generation of IL-1-beta by interacting with caspase-1 and preventing its association with RIP2. Down-regulates the release of IL1B.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (P57730), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
