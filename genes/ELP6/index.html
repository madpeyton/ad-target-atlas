ELP6

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000163832.

Predicted to be involved in positive regulation of cell migration. Located in cytosol and nucleus. Part of elongator holoenzyme complex. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Component of the elongator complex which is required for multiple tRNA modifications, including mcm5U (5-methoxycarbonylmethyl uridine), mcm5s2U (5-methoxycarbonylmethyl-2-thiouridine), and ncm5U (5-carbamoylmethyl uridine) (PubMed:29332244). The elongator complex catalyzes formation of carboxymethyluridine in the wobble base at position 34 in tRNAs (PubMed:22854966, PubMed:29332244). Involved in cell migration (By similarity).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q0PNE2), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
