RASGEF1B

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000138670.

Enables guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor activity. Predicted to be involved in regulation of catalytic activity and small GTPase mediated signal transduction. Predicted to be located in early endosome; late endosome; and midbody. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) with specificity for RAP2A, it doesn't seems to activate other Ras family proteins (in vitro).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q0VAM2), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 153020 · UniProt Q0VAM2 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
