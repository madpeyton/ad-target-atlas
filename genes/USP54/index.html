USP54

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000166348.

Predicted to enable thiol-dependent deubiquitinase. Predicted to be involved in protein deubiquitination. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Deubiquitinase that specifically mediates 'Lys-63'-linked deubiquitination of substrates with a polyubiquitin chain composed of at least 3 ubiquitins (PubMed:39587316). Specifically recognizes ubiquitin chain in position S2 and catalyzes cleavage of polyubiquitin within 'Lys-63'-linked chains (PubMed:39587316). Not able to deubiquitinate substrates with shorter ubiquitin chains (PubMed:39587316).

Show more (1 more sentence)

Mediates deubiquitination of PLK4, maintaining PLK4 stability by reducing its ubiquitination-mediated degradation (PubMed:36590171).
