DOCK11

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000147251.

Predicted to enable guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor activity. Predicted to be involved in several processes, including marginal zone B cell differentiation; positive regulation of GTPase activity; and positive regulation of filopodium assembly. Predicted to be located in cytosol. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Guanine nucleotide-exchange factor (GEF) that activates CDC42 by exchanging bound GDP for free GTP (PubMed:37342957). Required for marginal zone (MZ) B-cell development, is associated with early bone marrow B-cell development, MZ B-cell formation, MZ B-cell number and marginal metallophilic macrophages morphology (By similarity). Facilitates filopodia formation through the activation of CDC42 (PubMed:37342957).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q5JSL3), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
