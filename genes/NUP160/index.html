NUP160

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000030066.

NUP160 is 1 of up to 60 proteins that make up the 120-MD nuclear pore complex, which mediates nucleoplasmic transport.[supplied by OMIM, Apr 2004]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Functions as a component of the nuclear pore complex (NPC) (PubMed:11564755, PubMed:11684705). Involved in poly(A)+ RNA transport.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q12769), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
