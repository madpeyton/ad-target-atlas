SIMC1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000170085.

Enables SUMO polymer binding activity and peptidase inhibitor activity. Predicted to be involved in negative regulation of peptidase activity. Located in sarcomere. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Plays a role in SMC5-SMC6 complex recruitment for viral restriction. Forms a complex with SLF2 and this complex is required to recruit SMC5-SMC6 complex to PML nuclear bodies and sites of viral replication. [Isoform 1]: Inhibits the protease activity of CAPN3.
