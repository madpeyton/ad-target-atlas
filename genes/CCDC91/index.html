CCDC91

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000123106.

Predicted to enable identical protein binding activity. Involved in Golgi to lysosome transport. Located in nucleoplasm and trans-Golgi network. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Involved in the regulation of membrane traffic through the trans-Golgi network (TGN). Functions in close cooperation with the GGAs in the sorting of hydrolases to lysosomes.
