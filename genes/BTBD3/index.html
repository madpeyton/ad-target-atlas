BTBD3

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000132640.

Enables identical protein binding activity. Predicted to be involved in cerebral cortex development and dendrite morphogenesis. Predicted to be located in nucleus. Predicted to be active in cytosol. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Acts as a key regulator of dendritic field orientation during development of sensory cortex. Also directs dendrites toward active axon terminals when ectopically expressed (By similarity).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q9Y2F9), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
