CHSY3

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000198108.

CSS3 is a glycosyltransferase that has both glucuronyltransferase and N-acetylgalactosaminyltransferase activities (Yada et al., 2003 [PubMed 12907687]).[supplied by OMIM, Mar 2008].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Catalytic component of CHSY1-CHPF2 and CHSY1-CHPF chondroitin sulfate synthase complexes (PubMed:41298522). Has both beta-1,3-glucuronic acid and beta-1,4-N-acetylgalactosamine transferase activity. Transfers glucuronic acid (GlcUA) from UDP-GlcUA and N-acetylgalactosamine (GalNAc) from UDP-GalNAc to the non-reducing end of the elongating chondroitin polymer.
