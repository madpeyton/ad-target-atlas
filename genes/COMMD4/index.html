COMMD4

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000140365.

Located in cytosol; intracellular membrane-bounded organelle; and plasma membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Scaffold protein in the commander complex that is essential for endosomal recycling of transmembrane cargos; the commander complex is composed of the CCC subcomplex and the retriever subcomplex (PubMed:37172566, PubMed:38459129). May modulate activity of cullin-RING E3 ubiquitin ligase (CRL) complexes (PubMed:21778237). Down-regulates activation of NF-kappa-B (PubMed:23637203).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9H0A8), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
