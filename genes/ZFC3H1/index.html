ZFC3H1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000133858.

Predicted to enable metal ion binding activity. Predicted to be involved in RNA processing. Located in nucleus. Part of exosome (RNase complex). [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Subunit of the trimeric poly(A) tail exosome targeting (PAXT) complex, a complex that directs a subset of long and polyadenylated poly(A) RNAs for exosomal degradation. The RNA exosome is fundamental for the degradation of RNA in eukaryotic nuclei. Substrate targeting is facilitated by its cofactor MTREX, which links to RNA-binding protein adapters.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (O60293), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
