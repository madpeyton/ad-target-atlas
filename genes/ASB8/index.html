ASB8

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000177981.

Predicted to be involved in intracellular signal transduction and protein ubiquitination. Predicted to be located in cytosol. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

May be a substrate-recognition component of a SCF-like ECS (Elongin-Cullin-SOCS-box protein) E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase complex which mediates the ubiquitination and subsequent proteasomal degradation of target proteins. Inhibits IFN-beta production through the IRF3 signaling pathway by targeting TBK1 via 'Lys-48'-linked ubiquitination, leading to its proteasomal degradation (PubMed:32298923).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9H765), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
