FAM168A

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000054965.

Involved in positive regulation of base-excision repair. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

In cancer context, decreases POLB ubiquitination and stabilizes its protein levels (PubMed:25260657). Acts, at least in part, through PI3K/AKT/NFKB signaling pathway and by preventing POLB degradation (PubMed:23251525, PubMed:25260657). Results in protection of cells from induced-DNA damage and apoptosis (PubMed:21334329, PubMed:23251525, PubMed:25260657).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q92567), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 23201 · UniProt Q92567 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
