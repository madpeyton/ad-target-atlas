HNRNPA3

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000170144.

Enables RNA binding activity. Predicted to be involved in mRNA splicing, via spliceosome. Located in nucleoplasm. Part of catalytic step 2 spliceosome. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Plays a role in cytoplasmic trafficking of RNA. Binds to the cis-acting response element, A2RE. May be involved in pre-mRNA splicing.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (P51991), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
