ARV1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000173409.

this gene encodes a transmembrane protein that contains a conserved zinc ribbon motif at the N- terminus. A similar protein in mouse is thought to function in fatty acid homeostasis. Mutations in this gene are associated with early infantile epileptic encephalopathy 38. [provided by RefSeq, Nov 2016].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Plays a role as a mediator in the endoplasmic reticulum (ER) cholesterol and bile acid homeostasis (PubMed:11063737, PubMed:12145310, PubMed:20663892). Participates in sterol transport out of the ER and distribution into plasma membranes (PubMed:20663892).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9H2C2), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 64801 · UniProt Q9H2C2 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
