TTYH3

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000136295.

This gene encodes a member of the tweety family of proteins. Members of this family function as chloride anion channels. The encoded protein functions as a calcium(2+)-activated large conductance chloride(-) channel. [provided by RefSeq, Jul 2008].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Calcium-independent, swelling-dependent volume-regulated anion channel (VRAC-swell) which plays a pivotal role in the process of regulatory volume decrease (RVD) in the brain through the efflux of anions like chloride and organic osmolytes like glutamate (By similarity). Probable large-conductance Ca(2+)-activated chloride channel (PubMed:15010458).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9C0H2), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
