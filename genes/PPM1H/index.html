PPM1H

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000111110.

Enables identical protein binding activity and phosphoprotein phosphatase activity. Predicted to be involved in protein dephosphorylation. Located in cytoplasm and nucleoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Dephosphorylates CDKN1B at 'Thr-187', thus removing a signal for proteasomal degradation.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q9ULR3), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
