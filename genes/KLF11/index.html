KLF11

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000172059.

The protein encoded by this gene is a zinc finger transcription factor that binds to SP1-like sequences in epsilon- and gamma-globin gene promoters. This binding inhibits cell growth and causes apoptosis. Defects in this gene are a cause of maturity-onset diabetes of the young type 7 (MODY7). Three transcript variants encoding two different isoforms have been found for this gene. [provided by RefSeq, Apr 2010].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Transcription factor (PubMed:10207080, PubMed:9748269). Activates the epsilon- and gamma-globin gene promoters and, to a much lower degree, the beta-globin gene and represses promoters containing SP1-like binding inhibiting cell growth (PubMed:10207080, PubMed:16131492, PubMed:9748269). Represses transcription of SMAD7 which enhances TGF-beta signaling (By similarity).
