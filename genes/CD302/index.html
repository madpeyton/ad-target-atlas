CD302

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000241399.

CD302 is a C-type lectin receptor involved in cell adhesion and migration, as well as endocytosis and phagocytosis (Kato et al., 2007 [PubMed 17947679]).[supplied by OMIM, Aug 2008].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Multifunctional C-type lectin receptor involved in endocytosis, phagocytosis and regulation of cell adhesion and migration (PubMed:17947679, PubMed:27316686). Plays a critical role in guiding dendritic cells to lymph nodes (PubMed:27316686). Also functions as a restriction factor for Hepatitis C virus (HCV) at the liver cell surface, likely by inhibiting a viral cell entry step (PubMed:35297672).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q8IX05), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
