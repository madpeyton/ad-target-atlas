MPDZ

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000107186.

The protein encoded by this gene has multiple PDZ domains, which are hallmarks of protein-protein interactions. The encoded protein is known to interact with the HTR2C receptor and may cause it to clump at the cell surface. Multiple transcript variants encoding different isoforms have been found for this gene. [provided by RefSeq, Aug 2015].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Member of the NMDAR signaling complex that may play a role in control of AMPAR potentiation and synaptic plasticity in excitatory synapses (PubMed:11150294, PubMed:15312654). Promotes clustering of HT2RC at the cell surface (By similarity).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (O75970), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
