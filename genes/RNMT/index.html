RNMT

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000101654.

Enables RNA binding activity and mRNA (guanine-N7-)-methyltransferase activity. Involved in 7-methylguanosine mRNA capping. Located in fibrillar center and nucleoplasm. Part of mRNA cap binding activity complex; mRNA cap methyltransferase complex; and receptor complex. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Catalytic subunit of the mRNA-capping methyltransferase RNMT:RAMAC complex that methylates the N7 position of the added guanosine to the 5'-cap structure of mRNAs (PubMed:10347220, PubMed:11114884, PubMed:22099306, PubMed:27422871, PubMed:9705270, PubMed:9790902). Binds RNA containing 5'-terminal GpppC (PubMed:11114884).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (O43148), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 8731 · UniProt O43148 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
