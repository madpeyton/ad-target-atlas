AIG1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000146416.

Enables hydrolase activity. Involved in long-chain fatty acid catabolic process. Located in membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Hydrolyzes bioactive fatty-acid esters of hydroxy-fatty acids (FAHFAs), but not other major classes of lipids (PubMed:27018888). Show a preference for FAHFAs with branching distal from the carboxylate head group of the lipids (PubMed:27018888).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q9NVV5), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
