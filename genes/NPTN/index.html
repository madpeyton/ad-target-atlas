NPTN

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000156642.

This gene encodes a type I transmembrane protein belonging to the Ig superfamily. The protein is believed to be involved in cell-cell interactions or cell-substrate interactions. Alternative splicing results in multiple transcript variants. [provided by RefSeq, May 2009].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Accessory subunit of plasma membrane calcium-transporting ATPase ATP2B2/PMCA2 that stabilizes the transporter at the cell surface (By similarity). Also acts as a chaperone for ATP2B1; stabilizes ATP2B1 and increases its ATPase activity (PubMed:30190470). Probable homophilic and heterophilic cell adhesion molecule involved in long term potentiation at hippocampal excitatory synapses through activation of p38MAPK (By similarity).

May also regulate neurite outgrowth by activating the FGFR1 signaling pathway (By similarity). May play a role in synaptic plasticity (By similarity). Promotes localization of XKR8 at the cell membrane (PubMed:27503893).
