JTB

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000143543.

Enables protein kinase binding activity. Involved in mitotic cytokinesis and positive regulation of protein kinase activity. Located in cytoplasm and midbody. Colocalizes with centrosome and spindle. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Required for normal cytokinesis during mitosis. Plays a role in the regulation of cell proliferation. May be a component of the chromosomal passenger complex (CPC), a complex that acts as a key regulator of mitosis.

Show more (4 more sentences)

The CPC complex has essential functions at the centromere in ensuring correct chromosome alignment and segregation and is required for chromatin-induced microtubule stabilization and spindle assembly. Increases AURKB activity. Inhibits apoptosis induced by TGFB1 (By similarity). Overexpression induces swelling of mitochondria and reduces mitochondrial membrane potential (By similarity).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (O76095), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
