LMF1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000103227.

Involved in triglyceride metabolic process. Predicted to be integral component of membrane. Predicted to be active in endoplasmic reticulum membrane. Implicated in familial lipase maturation factor 1 deficiency. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Involved in the maturation of specific proteins in the endoplasmic reticulum. Required for maturation and transport of active lipoprotein lipase (LPL) through the secretory pathway. Each LMF1 molecule chaperones 50 or more molecules of LPL.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q96S06), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 64788 · UniProt Q96S06 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
