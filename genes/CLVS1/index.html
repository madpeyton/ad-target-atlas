CLVS1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000177182.

Enables phosphatidylinositol-3,5-bisphosphate binding activity. Predicted to be involved in lysosome organization. Located in endosome. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Required for normal morphology of late endosomes and/or lysosomes in neurons (By similarity). Binds phosphatidylinositol 3,5-bisphosphate (PtdIns(3,5)P2).
