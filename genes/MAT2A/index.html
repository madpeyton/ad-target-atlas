MAT2A

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000168906.

The protein encoded by this gene catalyzes the production of S-adenosylmethionine (AdoMet) from methionine and ATP. AdoMet is the key methyl donor in cellular processes. [provided by RefSeq, Jun 2011].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Catalyzes the formation of S-adenosylmethionine from methionine and ATP. The reaction comprises two steps that are both catalyzed by the same enzyme: formation of S-adenosylmethionine (AdoMet) and triphosphate, and subsequent hydrolysis of the triphosphate.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (P31153), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
