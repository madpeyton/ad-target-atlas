IQUB

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000164675.

Predicted to be involved in cilium assembly. Predicted to act upstream of or within smoothened signaling pathway. Predicted to be active in acrosomal vesicle and motile cilium. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Adapter protein that anchors the radial spoke 1 (RS1) complex to the A microtubule of outer doublet microtubules in axonemes (PubMed:36355624). The triple radial spokes (RS1, RS2 and RS3) are required to modulate beating of the sperm flagellum (PubMed:36355624). May play a role in inhibiting signaling via MAPK1/ERK2 and MAPK3/ERK1 (PubMed:36355624).

Additionally, may play a role in the functioning of cilia (By similarity). Not required for the functioning of tracheal or ependymal cilia (By similarity).
