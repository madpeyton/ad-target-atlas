QRSL1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000130348.

Enables glutaminyl-tRNA synthase (glutamine-hydrolyzing) activity. Involved in glutaminyl-tRNAGln biosynthesis via transamidation and mitochondrial translation. Located in mitochondrion. Part of glutamyl-tRNA(Gln) amidotransferase complex. Implicated in combined oxidative phosphorylation deficiency 40. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Catalytic subunit of the mitochondrial Glu-tRNA(Gln) amidotransferase complex GatCAB (Probable) (PubMed:19805282). GatCAB allows the formation of correctly charged Gln-tRNA(Gln) through the transamidation of misacylated Glu-tRNA(Gln) in the mitochondria. The reaction takes place in the presence of glutamine and ATP through an activated gamma-phospho-Glu-tRNA(Gln) (By similarity).

Show more (2 more sentences)

This subunit probably catalyzes the hydrolysis of the amido donor Gln, releasing ammonia that is sequestered within a channel in the GatCAB complex and made available to subunit B for downstream aminolysis of Glu-tRNA(Gln) (Probable). Required for the stability of GATB (By similarity).
