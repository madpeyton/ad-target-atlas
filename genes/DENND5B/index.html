DENND5B

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000170456.

Enables guanyl-nucleotide exchange factor activity. Predicted to be involved in positive regulation of triglyceride transport and regulation of catalytic activity. Predicted to be located in cytosol. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) which may activate RAB39A and/or RAB39B. Promotes the exchange of GDP to GTP, converting inactive GDP-bound Rab proteins into their active GTP-bound form.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q6ZUT9), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
