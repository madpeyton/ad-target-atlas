PPP1R21

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000162869.

Located in early endosome. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Component of the FERRY complex (Five-subunit Endosomal Rab5 and RNA/ribosome intermediary) (PubMed:37267905, PubMed:37267906). The FERRY complex directly interacts with mRNAs and RAB5A, and functions as a RAB5A effector involved in the localization and the distribution of specific mRNAs most likely by mediating their endosomal transport. The complex recruits mRNAs and ribosomes to early endosomes through direct mRNA-interaction (PubMed:37267905).

Show more (3 more sentences)

In the complex, PPP1R21 serves as a binding hub connecting all five complex subunits and mediating the binding to mRNA and early endosomes via RAB5A (PubMed:37267906). Putative regulator of protein phosphatase 1 (PP1) activity (PubMed:19389623). May play a role in the endosomal sorting process or in endosome maturation pathway (Probable) (PubMed:30520571).
