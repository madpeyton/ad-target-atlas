TRAPPC4

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000196655.

Involved in autophagy and endoplasmic reticulum to Golgi vesicle-mediated transport. Part of TRAPP complex. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Core component of the TRAPP complexes which has a function of guanine nucleotide exchange factor activity for Rab1 GTPase (Probable). Plays a role in vesicular transport from endoplasmic reticulum to Golgi and autophagy (PubMed:31794024). May play a role in dendrite postsynaptic membrane trafficking (By similarity).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q9Y296), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
