CEP20

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000133393. Also called: FOPNL (symbols used by the datasets).

Enables identical protein binding activity. Involved in cilium assembly. Located in centriolar satellite and nucleoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Involved in the biogenesis of cilia (PubMed:20551181). Required for the recruitment of PLK1 to centrosomes and S phase progression (PubMed:24018379).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q96NB1), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
