SMYD4

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000186532.

Predicted to enable metal ion binding activity and methyltransferase activity. Involved in heart development. Predicted to be located in cytoplasm and nucleus. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Protein-lysine N-methyltransferase. Monomethylates PRMT5, modulating its transcriptional activity (PubMed:38438251). May also act as a histone methyltransferase (By similarity).

Show more (2 more sentences)

Plays a critical role in cardiac development. Acts as a key epigenetic regulator of gene expression during cardiac development via its dual activities as a methyltransferase and negative regulator of HDAC1 (By similarity).
