BTBD8

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000189195.

Predicted to be involved in clathrin-dependent synaptic vesicle endocytosis; neuron projection development; and synaptic vesicle budding from endosome. Located in nucleoplasm. Colocalizes with AP-2 adaptor complex. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Involved in clathrin-mediated endocytosis at the synapse. Plays a role in neuronal development and in synaptic vesicle recycling in mature neurons, a process required for normal synaptic transmission.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q5XKL5), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
