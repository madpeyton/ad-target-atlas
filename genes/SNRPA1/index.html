SNRPA1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000131876.

Enables RNA binding activity. Involved in mRNA splicing, via spliceosome and spermatogenesis. Located in nuclear speck. Part of U2-type catalytic step 2 spliceosome and U2-type precatalytic spliceosome. Implicated in connective tissue disease. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Involved in pre-mRNA splicing as component of the spliceosome (PubMed:11991638, PubMed:27035939, PubMed:28076346, PubMed:28502770, PubMed:28781166, PubMed:32494006). Associated with sn-RNP U2, where it contributes to the binding of stem loop IV of U2 snRNA (PubMed:27035939, PubMed:32494006, PubMed:9716128).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (P09661), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
