TMEM87A

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000103978.

Involved in retrograde transport, endosome to Golgi. Located in Golgi cisterna membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Voltage- and pH-dependent, inwardly rectifying cation channel that mediates ion flux across the Golgi membrane, supporting luminal acidification necessary for normal Golgi morphology and function (PubMed:38992057). Implicated in normal touch sensitivity through the generation of mechanically activated currents (PubMed:32228863, PubMed:38422143). Could also be involved in cell mechanosensitivity regulating cell adhesion and migration (PubMed:32228863).

Show more (2 more sentences)

May also be involved in retrograde transport from endosomes to the trans-Golgi network (TGN) (PubMed:26157166). Contributes to hippocampal spatial and contextual memory by maintaining Golgi function and structural integrity in hippocampal neurons (By similarity).
