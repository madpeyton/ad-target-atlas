FAT3

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000165323.

Predicted to enable calcium ion binding activity. Predicted to be involved in cell-cell adhesion. Predicted to act upstream of or within several processes, including negative regulation of dendrite development; neuron migration; and retina layer formation. Predicted to be located in dendrite and plasma membrane. Predicted to be integral component of membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

May play a role in the interactions between neurites derived from specific subsets of neurons during development.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q8TDW7), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
