AREL1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000119682.

Enables ubiquitin-protein transferase activity. Involved in negative regulation of apoptotic process; protein ubiquitination; and ubiquitin-dependent protein catabolic process. Located in cytosol. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

E3 ubiquitin-protein ligase that catalyzes 'Lys-11'- or 'Lys-33'-linked polyubiquitin chains, with some preference for 'Lys-33' linkages (PubMed:25752577). E3 ubiquitin-protein ligases accept ubiquitin from an E2 ubiquitin-conjugating enzyme in the form of a thioester and then directly transfers the ubiquitin to targeted substrates (PubMed:23479728, PubMed:31578312). Ubiquitinates SEPTIN4, DIABLO/SMAC and HTRA2 in vitro (PubMed:23479728).

Modulates pulmonary inflammation by targeting SOCS2 for ubiquitination and subsequent degradation by the proteasome (PubMed:31578312).
