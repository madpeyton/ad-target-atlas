CLSTN2

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000158258.

Predicted to enable calcium ion binding activity. Predicted to be involved in positive regulation of synapse assembly and positive regulation of synaptic transmission. Predicted to be located in postsynaptic density. Predicted to be active in cell surface; glutamatergic synapse; and postsynaptic membrane. Predicted to be integral component of postsynaptic density membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Postsynaptic adhesion molecule that binds to presynaptic neurexins to mediate synapse formation, and which is involved in learning and memory (By similarity). Promotes synapse development by acting as a cell adhesion molecule at the postsynaptic membrane, which associates with neurexin-alpha at the presynaptic membrane (By similarity).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9H4D0), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
