ETAA1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000143971.

Enables protein serine/threonine kinase activator activity. Involved in several processes, including positive regulation of protein serine/threonine kinase activity; regulation of DNA damage checkpoint; and replication fork processing. Located in cytosol; nuclear replication fork; and nucleoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Replication stress response protein that accumulates at DNA damage sites and promotes replication fork progression and integrity (PubMed:27601467, PubMed:27723717, PubMed:27723720). Recruited to stalled replication forks via interaction with the RPA complex and directly stimulates ATR kinase activity independently of TOPBP1 (PubMed:27723717, PubMed:27723720, PubMed:30139873). Probably only regulates a subset of ATR targets (PubMed:27723717, PubMed:27723720).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q9NY74), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
