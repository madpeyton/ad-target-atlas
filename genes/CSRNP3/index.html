CSRNP3

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000178662.

Predicted to enable DNA-binding transcription factor activity, RNA polymerase II-specific and sequence-specific DNA binding activity. Predicted to be involved in positive regulation of apoptotic process and positive regulation of transcription by RNA polymerase II. Predicted to be part of chromatin.
