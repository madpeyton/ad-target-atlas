PRXL2A

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000122378. Also called: FAM213A (symbols used by the datasets).

Enables antioxidant activity. Involved in regulation of osteoclast differentiation. Located in cytoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Involved in redox regulation of the cell (PubMed:19951071, PubMed:26438880). Acts as an antioxidant (PubMed:19951071, PubMed:26438880). Inhibits TNFSF11-induced NFKB1 and JUN activation and osteoclast differentiation (PubMed:19951071).

Show more (2 more sentences)

May affect bone resorption and help to maintain bone mass (PubMed:19951071). Acts as a negative regulator of macrophage-mediated inflammation by inhibiting macrophage production of inflammatory cytokines, probably through suppression of the MAPK signaling pathway (PubMed:26438880).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9BRX8), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
