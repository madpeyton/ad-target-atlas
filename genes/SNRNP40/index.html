SNRNP40

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000060688.

This gene encodes a component of the U5 small nuclear ribonucleoprotein (snRNP) particle. The U5 snRNP is part of the spliceosome, a multiprotein complex that catalyzes the removal of introns from pre-messenger RNAs. [provided by RefSeq, Jul 2008].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Required for pre-mRNA splicing as component of the activated spliceosome (PubMed:11991638, PubMed:28076346, PubMed:28502770, PubMed:28781166, PubMed:29301961, PubMed:29360106, PubMed:30315277, PubMed:30705154). Component of the U5 small nuclear ribonucleoprotein (snRNP) complex and the U4/U6-U5 tri-snRNP complex, building blocks of the spliceosome (PubMed:16723661, PubMed:26912367, PubMed:9774689). As a component of the minor spliceosome, involved in the splicing of U12-type introns in pre-mRNAs (Probable).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q96DI7), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
