SNCG

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000173267.

This gene encodes a member of the synuclein family of proteins which are believed to be involved in the pathogenesis of neurodegenerative diseases. Mutations in this gene have also been associated with breast tumor development. [provided by RefSeq, Jan 2010].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Plays a role in neurofilament network integrity. May be involved in modulating axonal architecture during development and in the adult. In vitro, increases the susceptibility of neurofilament-H to calcium-dependent proteases (By similarity).
