EVL

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000196405.

Predicted to enable SH3 domain binding activity and profilin binding activity. Involved in negative regulation of epithelial cell migration; negative regulation of ruffle assembly; and positive regulation of stress fiber assembly. Located in membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Ena/VASP proteins are actin-associated proteins involved in a range of processes dependent on cytoskeleton remodeling and cell polarity such as axon guidance and lamellipodial and filopodial dynamics in migrating cells. EVL enhances actin nucleation and polymerization.
