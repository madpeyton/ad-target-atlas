MAN1C1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000117643.

Predicted to enable mannosyl-oligosaccharide 1,2-alpha-mannosidase activity. Predicted to be involved in N-glycan processing. Located in extracellular exosome. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Involved in the maturation of Asn-linked oligosaccharides. Trim alpha-1,2-linked mannose residues from Man(9)GlcNAc(2) to produce first Man(8)GlcNAc(2) then Man(6)GlcNAc and a small amount of Man(5)GlcNAc.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9NR34), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
