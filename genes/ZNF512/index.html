ZNF512

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000243943.

This gene encodes a protein containing four putative zinc finger motifs. Zinc finger motifs may bind to proteins or nucleic acids. Zinc finger-containing proteins are involved in a variety of processes, including regulation of transcription. Alternative splicing results in multiple transcript variants for this gene. [provided by RefSeq, Sep 2012].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Sequence-specific DNA-binding protein that recognizes repetitive and non-consecutive TTC sequences in pericentric repeat and initiates heterochromatin formation through interaction with SUV39H1/2 methyltransferases which catalyze histone H3K9 methylation.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q96ME7), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
