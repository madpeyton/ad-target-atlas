FOCAD

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000188352.

Located in focal adhesion. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Required for the maintenance of SKIC2 and SKIC3 proteostatic levels in the liver. May be involved in the regulation of RNA degradation by the exosome complex (PubMed:35864190). Potential tumor suppressor in gliomas.
