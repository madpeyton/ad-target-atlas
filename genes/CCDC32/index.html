CCDC32

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000128891. Also called: C15orf57 (symbols used by the datasets).

Involved in head development. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Regulates clathrin-mediated endocytsois of cargos such as transferrin probably through the association and modulation of adaptor protein complex 2 (AP-2) (PubMed:33859415). Has a role in ciliogenesis (By similarity). Required for proper cephalic and left/right axis development (PubMed:32307552).
