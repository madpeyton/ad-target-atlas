MTUS2

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000132938.

Enables microtubule binding activity and protein homodimerization activity. Part of nucleus. Colocalizes with centrosome and cytoplasmic microtubule. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Binds microtubules. Together with MAPRE1 may target the microtubule depolymerase KIF2C to the plus-end of microtubules. May regulate the dynamics of microtubules at their growing distal tip.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q5JR59), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 23281 · UniProt Q5JR59 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
