TOMM22

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000100216.

The protein encoded by this gene is an integral membrane protein of the mitochondrial outer membrane. The encoded protein interacts with TOMM20 and TOMM40, and forms a complex with several other proteins to import cytosolic preproteins into the mitochondrion. [provided by RefSeq, Jul 2008].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Central receptor component of the translocase of the outer membrane of mitochondria (TOM) complex essential for the recognition and translocation of cytosolically synthesized mitochondrial preproteins (PubMed:40080546). Together with the peripheral receptor TOMM20, functions as the transit peptide receptor and facilitates the movement of preproteins into the translocation pore (PubMed:10982837). The TOM complex associates with the ion channel VDAC2 and PINK1 kinase at depolarized mitochondria, this interaction stabilizes PINK1 at the outer mitochondrial membrane and triggers downstream mitophagy by the recruitment of the E3 ubiquitin ligase PRKN (PubMed:40080546).
