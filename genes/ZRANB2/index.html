ZRANB2

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000132485.

Enables RNA binding activity. Predicted to be involved in RNA splicing and mRNA processing. Located in nucleoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Splice factor required for alternative splicing of TRA2B/SFRS10 transcripts. Binds to ssRNA containing the consensus sequence 5'-AGGUAA-3' (PubMed:21256132). May interfere with constitutive 5'-splice site selection.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (O95218), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
