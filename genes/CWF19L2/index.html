CWF19L2

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000152404.

Predicted to be involved in mRNA splicing, via spliceosome. Predicted to be part of post-mRNA release spliceosomal complex. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

RNA-binding protein which regulates alternative splicing (By similarity). Recognizes the 5'-GG[A/C][A/G]G[A/C/G]-3' motif with 58% of target sequences containing a 5'-GGCAGA-3' motif (By similarity). Directly binds and regulates the splicing of genes related to spermatogenesis such as ZNHIT1, BTRC and FBXW7 and is thus required for male fertility (By similarity).

Show more (3 more sentences)

Also has an indirect effect on splicing by acting on the splicing factor RBFOX1 (By similarity). Required for spliceosome assembly and stability (By similarity). Required for debranching of intron-derived lariat RNAs (PubMed:41422678).
