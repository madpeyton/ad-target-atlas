CRYL1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000165475.

The uronate cycle functions as an alternative glucose metabolic pathway, accounting for about 5% of daily glucose catabolism. The product of this gene catalyzes the dehydrogenation of L-gulonate into dehydro-L-gulonate in the uronate cycle. The enzyme requires NAD(H) as a coenzyme, and is inhibited by inorganic phosphate. A similar gene in the rabbit is thought to serve a structural role in the lens of the eye. [provided by RefSeq, Jul 2008].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Catalyzes the conversion of L-gulonate to 3-dehydro-L-gulonate (PubMed:15809331, PubMed:40737316). It also exhibits low dehydrogenase activity toward L-3-hydroxybutyrate (HBA) and L-threonate (PubMed:15809331). Utilizes NAD as the sole cosubstrate and shows no activity with NADP (PubMed:40737316).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9Y2S2), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
