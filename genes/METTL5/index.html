METTL5

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000138382.

Enables S-adenosyl-L-methionine binding activity and rRNA (adenine-N6-)-methyltransferase activity. Involved in positive regulation of translation and rRNA methylation. Located in nucleus; postsynapse; and presynapse. Implicated in autosomal recessive intellectual developmental disorder-72. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Catalytic subunit of a heterodimer with TRMT112, which specifically methylates the 6th position of adenine in position 1832 of 18S rRNA (PubMed:31328227, PubMed:32217665, PubMed:33357433, PubMed:33428944, PubMed:35033535). N6-methylation of adenine(1832) in 18S rRNA resides in the decoding center of 18S rRNA and is required for translation and embryonic stem cells (ESCs) pluripotency and differentiation (PubMed:33357433).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9NRN9), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
