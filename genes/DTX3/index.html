DTX3

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000178498.

DTX3 functions as an E3 ubiquitin ligase (Takeyama et al., 2003 [PubMed 12670957]).[supplied by OMIM, Nov 2009].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Functions as an ubiquitin ligase protein, and regulates Notch signaling (PubMed:35782979, PubMed:40127622). By promoting the ubiquitination and subsequent degradation of target proteins, it can block activation of the Notch signaling pathway, potentially acting as a tumor suppressor in human cancers (PubMed:31854042, PubMed:35782979, PubMed:40127622). [Isoform 3]: Involved in the angiogenic EPHB4 kinase degradation in cancer cell.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q8N9I9), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
