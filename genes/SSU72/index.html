Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000160075.

Enables RNA polymerase II CTD heptapeptide repeat phosphatase activity. Involved in dephosphorylation of RNA polymerase II C-terminal domain and mRNA polyadenylation. Located in cytosol and nucleoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Protein phosphatase that catalyzes the dephosphorylation of the C-terminal domain of RNA polymerase II. Plays a role in RNA processing and termination. Plays a role in pre-mRNA polyadenylation via its interaction with SYMPK.
