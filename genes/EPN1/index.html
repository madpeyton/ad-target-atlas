EPN1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000063245.

This gene encodes a member of the epsin protein family. The encoded protein binds clathrin and is involved in the endocytosis of clathrin-coated vesicles. Loss of function of this gene is associated with reduced tumor growth and progression in certain cancer types. [provided by RefSeq, Mar 2016].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Binds to membranes enriched in phosphatidylinositol 4,5-bisphosphate (PtdIns(4,5)P2). Modifies membrane curvature and facilitates the formation of clathrin-coated invaginations (By similarity). Regulates receptor-mediated endocytosis (PubMed:10393179, PubMed:10557078).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9Y6I3), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
