MT-ND3

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000198840.

Enables NADH dehydrogenase (ubiquinone) activity. Involved in mitochondrial electron transport, NADH to ubiquinone. Part of mitochondrial respiratory chain complex I. Implicated in Leber hereditary optic neuropathy; Leigh disease; and Parkinson's disease. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Core subunit of the mitochondrial membrane respiratory chain NADH dehydrogenase (Complex I) which catalyzes electron transfer from NADH through the respiratory chain, using ubiquinone as an electron acceptor (PubMed:25118196). Essential for the catalytic activity of complex I (PubMed:25118196).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (P03897), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
