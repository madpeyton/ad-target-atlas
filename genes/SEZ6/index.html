SEZ6

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000063015.

The protein encoded by this gene is thought to contain five cysteine-rich motifs that are similar to sushi domains, as well as two domains similar to the amino terminal half of the CUB (for complement C1r/C1s, Uegf, Bmp1) domain. Mutations in this gene have been associated with febrile seizures. [provided by RefSeq, Jul 2016].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

May play a role in cell-cell recognition and in neuronal membrane signaling. Seems to be important for the achievement of the necessary balance between dendrite elongation and branching during the elaboration of a complex dendritic arbor. Involved in the development of appropriate excitatory synaptic connectivity (By similarity).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q53EL9), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 124925 · UniProt Q53EL9 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
