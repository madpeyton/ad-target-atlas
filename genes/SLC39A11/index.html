SLC39A11

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000133195.

Predicted to enable zinc ion transmembrane transporter activity. Predicted to be involved in zinc ion transmembrane transport. Predicted to be located in Golgi apparatus; nucleus; and plasma membrane. Predicted to be active in membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Zinc importer that regulates cytosolic zinc concentrations either via zinc influx from the extracellular compartment or efflux from intracellular organelles such as Golgi apparatus. May transport copper ions as well. The transport mechanism remains to be elucidated.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q8N1S5), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 201266 · UniProt Q8N1S5 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
