COX16

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000133983.

Involved in mitochondrial cytochrome c oxidase assembly. Is integral component of mitochondrial inner membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Required for the assembly of the mitochondrial respiratory chain complex IV (CIV), also known as cytochrome c oxidase (PubMed:29355485, PubMed:29381136, PubMed:33169484). Promotes the insertion of copper into the active site of cytochrome c oxidase subunit II (MT-CO2/COX2) (PubMed:29355485, PubMed:29381136). Interacts specifically with newly synthesized MT-CO2/COX and its copper center-forming metallochaperones SCO1, SCO2 and COA6 (PubMed:29381136).

Show more (1 more sentence)

Probably facilitates MT-CO2/COX2 association with the MITRAC assembly intermediate containing MT-CO1/COX1, thereby participating in merging the MT-CO1/COX1 and MT-CO2/COX2 assembly lines (PubMed:29381136).
