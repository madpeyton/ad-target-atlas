YY1AP1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000163374.

The encoded gene product presumably interacts with YY1 protein; however, its exact function is not known. Alternative splicing results in multiple transcript variants encoding different isoforms. [provided by RefSeq, Jul 2008]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Associates with the INO80 chromatin remodeling complex, which is responsible for transcriptional regulation, DNA repair, and replication (PubMed:27939641). Enhances transcription activation by YY1 (PubMed:14744866). Plays a role in cell cycle regulation (PubMed:17541814, PubMed:27939641).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9H869), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
