ISY1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000240682.

Enables RNA binding activity. Involved in mRNA splicing, via spliceosome. Located in nucleus. Part of U2-type catalytic step 1 spliceosome and catalytic step 2 spliceosome. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Component of the spliceosome C complex required for the selective processing of microRNAs during embryonic stem cell differentiation (By similarity). Required for the biogenesis of all miRNAs from the pri-miR-17-92 primary transcript except miR-92a (By similarity). Only required for the biogenesis of miR-290 and miR-96 from the pri-miR-290-295 and pri-miR-96-183 primary transcripts, respectively (By similarity).

Show more (3 more sentences)

Required during the transition of embryonic stem cells (ESCs) from the naive to primed state (By similarity). By enhancing miRNA biogenesis, promotes exit of ESCs from the naive state to an intermediate state of poised pluripotency, which precedes transition to the primed state (By similarity). Involved in pre-mRNA splicing as component of the spliceosome.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9ULR0), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
