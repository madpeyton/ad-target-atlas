Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000088899. Also called: RP5-1187M17.10 (symbols used by the datasets).

Predicted to enable PDZ domain binding activity and protein self-association. Predicted to be involved in protein homooligomerization and regulation of dendritic spine morphogenesis. Predicted to be located in postsynaptic density. Predicted to be active in dendritic spine. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.
