TMBIM4

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000155957.

Involved in negative regulation of apoptotic process and regulation of calcium-mediated signaling. Located in Golgi stack. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Anti-apoptotic protein which can inhibit apoptosis induced by intrinsic and extrinsic apoptotic stimuli. Can modulate both capacitative Ca2+ entry and inositol 1,4,5-trisphosphate (IP3)-mediated Ca2+ release.
