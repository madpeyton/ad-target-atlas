RWDD1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000111832.

Predicted to be involved in several processes, including cellular response to lipid; cytoplasmic translation; and positive regulation of androgen receptor activity. Predicted to be located in cytoplasm. Predicted to be part of polysome. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.
