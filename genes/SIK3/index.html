SIK3

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000160584.

Enables ATP binding activity; magnesium ion binding activity; and protein serine/threonine kinase activity. Involved in positive regulation of TORC1 signaling; positive regulation of TORC2 signaling; and protein phosphorylation. Located in nucleoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Positive regulator of mTOR signaling that functions by triggering the degradation of DEPTOR, an mTOR inhibitor. Involved in the dynamic regulation of mTOR signaling in chondrocyte differentiation during skeletogenesis (PubMed:30232230). Negatively regulates cAMP signaling pathway possibly by acting on CRTC2/TORC2 and CRTC3/TORC3 (Probable).
