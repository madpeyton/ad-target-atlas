DOCK9

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000088387.

Enables cadherin binding activity. Predicted to be involved in positive regulation of GTPase activity. Located in membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Guanine nucleotide-exchange factor (GEF) that activates CDC42 by exchanging bound GDP for free GTP. Overexpression induces filopodia formation.
