ZNF277

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000198839.

Predicted to enable RNA polymerase II cis-regulatory region sequence-specific DNA binding activity and metal ion binding activity. Predicted to act upstream of or within cellular response to hydrogen peroxide and regulation of cellular senescence. Predicted to be located in nucleus. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Probable transcription factor. Involved in modulation of cellular senescence; represses transcription of the tumor suppressor gene INK4A/ARF, perhaps acting via the Polycomb group (PcG) complex PRC1.
