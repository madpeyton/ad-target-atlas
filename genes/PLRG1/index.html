PLRG1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000171566.

This gene encodes a core component of the cell division cycle 5-like (CDC5L) complex. The CDC5L complex is part of the spliceosome and is required for pre-mRNA splicing. The encoded protein plays a critical role in alternative splice site selection. Alternatively spliced transcript variants encoding multiple isoforms have been observed for this gene. [provided by RefSeq, Jan 2011].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Involved in pre-mRNA splicing as component of the spliceosome (PubMed:28076346, PubMed:28502770). Component of the PRP19-CDC5L complex that forms an integral part of the spliceosome and is required for activating pre-mRNA splicing (PubMed:11101529, PubMed:11544257). As a component of the minor spliceosome, involved in the splicing of U12-type introns in pre-mRNAs (Probable).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (O43660), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
