TTC7B

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000165914.

Involved in phosphatidylinositol phosphate biosynthetic process and protein localization to plasma membrane. Located in cytosol and plasma membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Component of a complex required to localize phosphatidylinositol 4-kinase (PI4K) to the plasma membrane. The complex acts as a regulator of phosphatidylinositol 4-phosphate (PtdIns(4)P) synthesis. In the complex, plays a central role in bridging PI4KA to EFR3B and HYCC1, via direct interactions (PubMed:26571211).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q86TV6), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
