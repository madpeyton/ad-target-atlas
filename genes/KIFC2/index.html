KIFC2

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000167702.

Predicted to enable microtubule binding activity and microtubule motor activity. Predicted to be involved in microtubule-based movement and mitotic spindle assembly. Predicted to be located in cytoplasm. Predicted to be part of kinesin complex. Predicted to be active in microtubule cytoskeleton and nucleus. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

May play a role in microtubule-dependent retrograde axonal transport. May function as the motor for the transport of multivesicular body (MVB)-like organelles in dendrites (By similarity).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q96AC6), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
