HNRNPUL1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000105323.

This gene encodes a nuclear RNA-binding protein of the heterogeneous nuclear ribonucleoprotein (hnRNP) family. This protein binds specifically to adenovirus early-1B-55kDa oncoprotein. It may play an important role in nucleocytoplasmic RNA transport, and its function is modulated by early-1B-55kDa in adenovirus-infected cells. [provided by RefSeq, Mar 2016].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Acts as a basic transcriptional regulator (PubMed:12489984). Represses basic transcription driven by several virus and cellular promoters (PubMed:12489984). When associated with BRD7, activates transcription of glucocorticoid-responsive promoter in the absence of ligand-stimulation (PubMed:12489984).

Also plays a role in mRNA processing and transport (PubMed:9733834). Binds avidly to poly(G) and poly(C) RNA homopolymers in vitro (PubMed:9733834).
