NUP42

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000136243. Also called: NUPL2 (symbols used by the datasets).

Enables nuclear export signal receptor activity. Involved in protein export from nucleus. Located in cytosol and nucleoplasm. Colocalizes with nuclear envelope. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Required for the export of mRNAs containing poly(A) tails from the nucleus into the cytoplasm. (Microbial infection) In case of infection by HIV-1, it may participate in the docking of viral Vpr at the nuclear envelope.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (O15504), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 11097 · UniProt O15504 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
