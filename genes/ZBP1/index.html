ZBP1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000124256.

This gene encodes a Z-DNA binding protein. The encoded protein plays a role in the innate immune response by binding to foreign DNA and inducing type-I interferon production. Alternatively spliced transcript variants encoding multiple isoforms have been observed for this gene. [provided by RefSeq, Dec 2011].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-11-06.

Key innate sensor that recognizes and binds left-handed double-stranded Z-RNA structures, which are produced by a number of viruses, such as herpesvirus, orthomyxovirus or flavivirus, and triggers different forms of programmed cell death (PubMed:32200799). Z-RNA structures can also be derived from endogenous RNAs, such as retroelements, Alu duplex RNAs or arise from aberrant transcription or splicing events (PubMed:40267921). Acts as a direct activator of necroptosis, a programmed cell death process, via its ability to bind Z-RNA: once activated upon Z-RNA-binding, ZBP1 interacts and stimulates RIPK3 kinase, which phosphorylates and activates MLKL, triggering execution of programmed necrosis via disruption of the plasma membrane (PubMed:32200799).

Necroptosis can also take place in the nucleus in response to orthomyxoviruses infection: ZBP1 recognizes and binds Z-RNA structures that are produced in infected nuclei by orthomyxoviruses, such as the influenza A virus (IAV), leading to ZBP1 activation, RIPK3 stimulation and subsequent MLKL phosphorylation, triggering disruption of the nuclear envelope and leakage of cellular DNA into the cytosol (PubMed:32200799). ZBP1 acts as an essential mediator of pyroptosis, apoptosis and necroptosis (PANoptosis), an integral part of host defense against pathogens, by activating the NLRP3 inflammasome, caspase-8 (CASP8) and RIPK3 (PubMed:41310329). Activates CASP8-mediated apoptosis via its interaction with RIPK1 but independently of its ability to sense Z-RNAs (By similarity). Involved in PANoptosis triggered in response to bacterial infection: component of the AIM2 PANoptosome complex, a multiprotein complex that triggers PANoptosis (By similarity). Also acts as the apical sensor of fungal infection responsible for activating PANoptosis (By similarity). PANoptosis can also be triggered in response to SARS-CoV-2 infection, leading to a cytokine storm: mechanistically, SARS-CoV-2 infection promotes ADAR degradation by STING1-mediated autophagy, resulting in the accumulation of Z-RNAs, which subsequently trigger ZBP1-dependent PANoptosis (PubMed:35587515, PubMed:36650286, PubMed:40627395). ZBP1-dependent cell death in response to IAV infection promotes interleukin-1 alpha (IL1A) induction in an NLRP3-inflammasome-independent manner: IL1A expression is required for the optimal interleukin-1 beta (IL1B) production, and together, these cytokines promote infiltration of inflammatory neutrophils to the lung, leading to the formation of neutrophil extracellular traps (By similarity). Also promotes programmed cell death in response to heat stress independently of Z-RNAs: induced by HSF1, leading to (1) activation of RIPK3 kinase and MLKL-dependent necroptosis and (2) CASP8-dependent apoptosis (By similarity). In some cell types, also able to restrict viral replication by promoting cell death-independent responses (By similarity). In response to Zika virus infection in neurons, promotes a cell death-independent pathway that restricts viral replication: together with RIPK3, promotes a death-independent transcriptional program that modifies the cellular metabolism via up-regulation expression of the enzyme ACOD1/IRG1 and production of the metabolite itaconate (By similarity). Itaconate inhibits the activity of succinate dehydrogenase, generating a metabolic state in neurons that suppresses replication of viral genomes (By similarity). [Isoform 7]: Innate immune sensor, which specifically recognizes and binds telomeric-repeat-containing RNAs (TERRA) that are synthesized from dysfunctional telomeres, promoting activation of MAVS (PubMed:36755096). Homooligomerizes into filaments on the outer mitochondrial membrane following activation by TERRA RNAs, where it activates MAVS (PubMed:36755096). (Microbial infection) In case of herpes simplex virus 1/HHV-1 infection, forms hetero-amyloid structures with HHV-1 protein RIR1/ICP6 which may inhibit ZBP1-mediated necroptosis, thereby preventing host cell death pathway and allowing viral evasion. (Microbial infection) Vaccinia virus OPG065 protein inhibits ZBP1-mediated necroptosis by binding to Z-RNAs and competing with Z-RNA detection by ZBP1.
