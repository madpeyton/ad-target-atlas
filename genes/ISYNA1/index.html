ISYNA1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000105655.

This gene encodes an inositol-3-phosphate synthase enzyme. The encoded protein plays a critical role in the myo-inositol biosynthesis pathway by catalyzing the rate-limiting conversion of glucose 6-phosphate to myoinositol 1-phosphate. Alternatively spliced transcript variants encoding multiple isoforms have been observed for this gene, and a pseudogene of this gene is located on the short arm of chromosome 4. [provided by RefSeq, Nov 2011].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Key enzyme in myo-inositol biosynthesis pathway that catalyzes the conversion of glucose 6-phosphate to 1-myo-inositol 1-phosphate in a NAD-dependent manner (PubMed:15024000, PubMed:23902760). Rate-limiting enzyme in the synthesis of all inositol-containing compounds (PubMed:15024000).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9NPH2), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
