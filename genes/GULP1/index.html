GULP1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000144366.

The protein encoded by this gene is an adapter protein necessary for the engulfment of apoptotic cells by phagocytes. Several transcript variants, some protein coding and some thought not to be protein coding, have been found for this gene. [provided by RefSeq, Nov 2011].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

May function as an adapter protein. Required for efficient phagocytosis of apoptotic cells. Modulates cellular glycosphingolipid and cholesterol transport.

Show more (2 more sentences)

May play a role in the internalization and endosomal trafficking of various LRP1 ligands, such as PSAP. Increases cellular levels of GTP-bound ARF6.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q9UBP9), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
