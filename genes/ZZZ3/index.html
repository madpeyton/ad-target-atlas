ZZZ3

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000036549. Also called: AC118549.1 (symbols used by the datasets).

Predicted to enable DNA binding activity and zinc ion binding activity. Predicted to be involved in histone H4 acetylation. Located in nucleolus and nucleoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Histone H3 reader that is required for the ATAC complex-mediated maintenance of histone acetylation and gene activation (PubMed:30217978). Component of the ATAC complex, a complex with histone acetyltransferase activity on histones H3 and H4 (PubMed:19103755).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q8IYH5), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
