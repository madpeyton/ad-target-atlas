PLEKHA2

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000169499.

Enables fibronectin binding activity; laminin binding activity; and phosphatidylinositol-3,4-bisphosphate binding activity. Involved in positive regulation of cell-matrix adhesion. Located in cytoplasm and membrane. Part of protein-containing complex. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Binds specifically to phosphatidylinositol 3,4-diphosphate (PtdIns3,4P2), but not to other phosphoinositides. May recruit other proteins to the plasma membrane (By similarity).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q9HB19), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 59339 · UniProt Q9HB19 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
