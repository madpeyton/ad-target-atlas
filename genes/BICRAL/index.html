BICRAL

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000112624. Also called: GLTSCR1L (symbols used by the datasets).

Predicted to be involved in positive regulation of transcription, DNA-templated. Part of SWI/SNF complex. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Component of SWI/SNF chromatin remodeling subcomplex GBAF that carries out key enzymatic activities, changing chromatin structure by altering DNA-histone contacts within a nucleosome in an ATP-dependent manner.
