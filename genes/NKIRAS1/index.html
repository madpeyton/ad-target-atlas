NKIRAS1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000197885.

Predicted to enable GTPase activating protein binding activity. Predicted to be involved in I-kappaB kinase/NF-kappaB signaling. Predicted to act upstream of or within several processes, including Ral protein signal transduction; lung alveolus development; and surfactant homeostasis. Located in cytosol and endoplasmic reticulum. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Atypical Ras-like protein that acts as a potent regulator of NF-kappa-B activity by preventing the degradation of NF-kappa-B inhibitor beta (NFKBIB) by most signals, explaining why NFKBIB is more resistant to degradation. May act by blocking phosphorylation of NFKBIB and mediating cytoplasmic retention of p65/RELA NF-kappa-B subunit. It is unclear whether it acts as a GTPase.

Both GTP- and GDP-bound forms block phosphorylation of NFKBIB.
