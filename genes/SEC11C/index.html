SEC11C

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000166562.

Predicted to enable peptidase activity. Predicted to be involved in signal peptide processing. Predicted to be located in endoplasmic reticulum membrane. Predicted to be part of signal peptidase complex. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Catalytic component of the signal peptidase complex (SPC) which catalyzes the cleavage of N-terminal signal sequences from nascent proteins as they are translocated into the lumen of the endoplasmic reticulum (PubMed:34388369). Specifically cleaves N-terminal signal peptides that contain a hydrophobic alpha-helix (h-region) shorter than 18-20 amino acids (PubMed:34388369).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9BY50), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
