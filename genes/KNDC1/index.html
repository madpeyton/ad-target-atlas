KNDC1

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000171798.

The protein encoded by this gene is a Ras guanine nucleotide exchange factor that appears to negatively regulate dendritic growth in the brain. Knockdown of this gene in senescent umbilical vein endothelial cells partially reversed the senescence, showing that this gene could potentially be targeted by anti-aging therapies. [provided by RefSeq, Dec 2016].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

RAS-Guanine nucleotide exchange factor (GEF) that controls the negative regulation of neuronal dendrite growth by mediating a signaling pathway linking RAS and MAP2 (By similarity). May be involved in cellular senescence (PubMed:24788352).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q76NI1), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
