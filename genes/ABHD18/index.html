ABHD18

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000164074.

Predicted to be located in extracellular region. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Catalyzes the hydrolysis of an acyl chain from the tetra-acylated cardiolipin (CL) and produces the tri-acylated monolysocardiolipin (MLCL) (PubMed:40903572). Prefers CL with shorter and saturated fatty acyl chains as substrates (PubMed:40903572). Together with Tafazzin (TAZ), it participates in the mechanism of CL remodeling, a process consisting of repetitive cycles of hydrolysis and reacylation of the fatty acids that make up CL, where the nascent acyl chains are replaced with longer, unsaturated chains (PubMed:40903572).
