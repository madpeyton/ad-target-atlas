HEATR5B

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000008869.

Predicted to be involved in endocytosis; protein localization; and retrograde transport, endosome to Golgi. Located in membrane. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Component of clathrin-coated vesicles (PubMed:15758025). Component of the aftiphilin/p200/gamma-synergin complex, which plays roles in AP1G1/AP-1-mediated protein trafficking including the trafficking of transferrin from early to recycling endosomes, and the membrane trafficking of furin and the lysosomal enzyme cathepsin D between the trans-Golgi network (TGN) and endosomes (PubMed:15758025).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9P2D3), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
