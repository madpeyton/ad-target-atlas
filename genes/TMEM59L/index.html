TMEM59L

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000105696.

This gene encodes a predicted type-I membrane glycoprotein. The encoded protein may play a role in functioning of the central nervous system. [provided by RefSeq, Jul 2008].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Modulates the O-glycosylation and complex N-glycosylation steps occurring during the Golgi maturation of APP. Inhibits APP transport to the cell surface and further shedding.

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (Q9UK28), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.

Links: NCBI Gene 25789 · UniProt Q9UK28 · Human Protein Atlas (brain) · GTEx · STRING
