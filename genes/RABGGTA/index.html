RABGGTA

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000100949.

Predicted to enable small GTPase binding activity. Predicted to contribute to Rab geranylgeranyltransferase activity. Predicted to be involved in protein geranylgeranylation. Located in nucleoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Catalyzes the transfer of a geranylgeranyl moiety from geranylgeranyl diphosphate to both cysteines of Rab proteins with the C-terminal sequence -XXCC, -XCXC and -CCXX, such as RAB1A, RAB3A, RAB5A and RAB7A.
