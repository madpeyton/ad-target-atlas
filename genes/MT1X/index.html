MT1X

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000187193.

Predicted to enable copper ion binding activity and zinc ion binding activity. Involved in cellular response to cadmium ion; cellular response to erythropoietin; and cellular response to zinc ion. Located in cytoplasm and nucleus. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Metallothioneins have a high content of cysteine residues that bind various heavy metals; these proteins are transcriptionally regulated by both heavy metals and glucocorticoids. May be involved in FAM168A anti-apoptotic signaling (PubMed:23251525).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_04 (P80297), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
