MTURN

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000180354.

Involved in negative regulation of NF-kappaB transcription factor activity; positive regulation of MAPK cascade; and positive regulation of megakaryocyte differentiation. Located in cytoplasm. [provided by Alliance of Genome Resources, Apr 2022]

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.

Promotes megakaryocyte differentiation by enhancing ERK and JNK signaling as well as up-regulating RUNX1 and FLI1 expression (PubMed:24681962). Represses NF-kappa-B transcriptional activity by inhibiting phosphorylation of RELA at 'Ser-536' (PubMed:24681962). May be involved in early neuronal development (By similarity).

Source: UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot Function, release 2026_03 (Q8N3F0), retrieved 2026-10-21; UniProt is licensed CC BY 4.0.
