PTENP1-AS

Ensembl gene ID: ENSG00000281128.

PTENP1-AS encodes an antisense RNA (asRNA) of PTENP1, a PTEN (phosphatase and tensin homolog) pseudogene. PTENP1 regulates PTEN by both sense (GeneID: 11191) and antisense RNAs. This antisense RNA (PTENP1-AS) regulates PTEN transcription and mRNA stability. [provided by RefSeq, Jun 2020].

Source: NCBI Gene summary (RefSeq), via MyGene.info, retrieved 2026-10-21.
