AD Target Atlas

Results from the TREAT-AD snRNA-seq pipeline

Gene and drug candidates for Alzheimer's disease (AD) from single-nucleus RNA sequencing of human prefrontal cortex, produced by the TREAT-AD v2 pipeline. For each cell type, genes are compared between AD and control donors with two tests: a nucleus-level Wilcoxon test (the main test) and a donor-level pseudobulk DESeq2 test; genes are grouped into co-expression modules (hdWGCNA), nuclei are scored for AD-likeness (DEGAS), and approved drugs are ranked against the protein structures of the top genes (DrugCLIP). These are computational predictions to guide follow-up experiments.

Drug targets: Green et al. 2024: in each cell type, the top 10 genes higher in AD in the nucleus-level test (Wilcoxon, step 11b), ranked by its test statistic, after removing mitochondrial and ribosomal protein genes. Mathys et al. 2019: in each cell type, the top 10 genes higher in AD in the nucleus-level test (Wilcoxon, step 11b), ranked by its test statistic, after removing mitochondrial and ribosomal protein genes. SEA-AD (Gabitto et al. 2024): in each cell type, the top 10 genes higher in AD in the nucleus-level test (Wilcoxon, step 11b), ranked by its test statistic, after removing mitochondrial and ribosomal protein genes. Approved drugs are scored against the targets' experimental structures (DrugCLIP, step 14b) and screening hits are taken from GenomeScreenDB (step 14a); see methods.
